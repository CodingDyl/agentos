# Anti-patterns

What makes a site read as generated. Every brief and the critique use this list; Impeccable's detector catches many of these on the rendered page too.

## Typography

- Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Poppins or Montserrat as the main face by default. Use the assigned pairing.
- One weight of one sans everywhere, with headings only slightly bigger than text.
- Centred body copy, or paragraphs wider than about 75 characters.
- Naming a typeface in DESIGN.md that the page does not actually load. AgentOS measures this in a browser.

## Colour

- Purple-to-blue or any decorative gradient on the hero, buttons or text.
- Navy plus gold for anything professional, or teal plus white for anything medical, unless the client's brand already is that.
- Glassmorphism, neon glows and soft drop shadows on every element.
- A warm cream or beige page as the reflex "tasteful" ground. Choose the ground on purpose; a paper colour needs its own reason.

## Layout

- Centred headline, sub-line and two buttons over a full-width photo, followed by an icon plus three cards grid.
- A small label, kicker or eyebrow above the headline. Delete it and let the headline speak.
- Copy on the left with an image or a boxed panel on the right, as a first instinct. It is the most repeated hero there is.
- A logo on the left, links on the right and a filled button in the corner, as the only idea for navigation. Follow the assigned navigation.
- Section numbers (01, 02, 03) unless the order itself is information the visitor needs.
- Cards inside cards, or every section as a rounded white card on a grey page.
- Identical section rhythm down the page: heading, sub-line, grid of three, repeat.
- A testimonial carousel, stats counter or logo wall when there is no real material for it.

## Imagery

- Stock-photo tropes: handshakes, gavels, scales of justice, smiling call-centre agents, a spanner crossed with a pipe, a tooth with a sparkle.
- Generic 3D blobs, abstract waves or isometric illustrations as decoration.

## Components

- Emoji or generic icon bullets in front of every list item.
- Pill buttons with an arrow icon on every call to action, and a floating chat bubble.
- Hard offset block shadows, unless the assigned direction is genuinely brutalist.
- A control that does nothing: a dead button, a # link, a form with no result. Anything that looks interactive works, on sample data if need be.

## Copy

- Vague claims: "Your trusted partner", "Excellence in every detail", "Solutions tailored to you", "We go the extra mile", "Quality you can trust".
- Headlines that could belong to any business in the niche. Name the real service, place or offer.
- Invented numbers, years, awards or reviews to fill a trust section.
