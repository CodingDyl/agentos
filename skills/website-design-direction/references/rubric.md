# Critique rubric

8 criteria, each scored 0 to 5: 40 in all. **28 passes.** A safe, average concept is a fail.

| # | Criterion | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Escapes the niche default (`niche`) | Nobody would guess the niche from the layout and palette alone, yet it fits the business. |
| 2 | Commits to its assigned direction (`direction`) | The direction's type, colour, layout grammar and imagery are all visible, not just one of them. |
| 3 | Typography (`type`) | A deliberate pairing with clear hierarchy and scale contrast; none of the default faces. |
| 4 | Colour (`colour`) | A clear colour logic, contrast of 4.5:1 or better for text, no decorative gradients. |
| 5 | Layout and composition (`layout`) | A composition specific to this concept; no centred hero plus three cards, no nested cards. |
| 6 | Copy specificity (`copy`) | Names the real service, place and next step; no vague claims, nothing invented. |
| 7 | Use of the client's brand (`brand`) | Logo, colours and photos used correctly where they exist, or a confident wordmark where they do not. |
| 8 | Craft and accessibility (`craft`) | Semantic landmarks, visible focus, works at 390px and 1440px, nothing broken or clipped. |

## Output format (hero critique)

```
## concept-a
Direction: <direction id>
Score: <total>/40
Scores: niche <n>, direction <n>, type <n>, colour <n>, layout <n>, copy <n>, brand <n>, craft <n>
Slop: <anti-patterns and clichés found, with where; or None>
Fix: <specific changes>
```

The build review adds one line after its verdict: `Design score: <total>/40`.
