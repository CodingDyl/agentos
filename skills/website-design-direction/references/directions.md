# Direction library

19 art directions: the look of a concept. AgentOS assigns each hero concept a different one; concept C is always a wildcard far from the niche. Study the reference, not other websites. Every face is open-licence and comes from Fontsource; AgentOS puts the files in the repo and the page must load them.

## Editorial broadsheet (`editorial-broadsheet`)

- **Reference:** Weekend newspaper front pages and long-read supplements
- **Type:** Newsreader / Public Sans; Piazzolla / Work Sans; Frank Ruhl Libre / Instrument Sans. A tall serif headline set tight, a dateline in small caps, body in a plain grotesque. Rules between columns, not boxes.
- **Colour (newsprint):** Cool grey newsprint ground, near-black ink, one spot colour used only for the call to action and a single rule. Starting point: #E2E1DC, #141414, #B3261E.
- **Layout grammar:** A masthead row, a multi-column grid with hairline rules, a lead story and a sidebar. Content density over whitespace.
- **Hero archetype:** `masthead-columns`
- **Imagery:** One documentary photograph with a caption, or none. Datelines and bylines as texture.

## Swiss grid (`swiss-grid`)

- **Reference:** International Typographic Style posters, 1950s to 1970s
- **Type:** Schibsted Grotesk / Schibsted Grotesk; Hanken Grotesk / Hanken Grotesk; Geologica / Geologica. One grotesque family in two or three weights, flush left, ragged right. Scale contrast does the work.
- **Colour (white-black-signal):** White ground, black type, a single saturated signal colour as a large flat field. Starting point: #FFFFFF, #0A0A0A, #FF4F00.
- **Layout grammar:** A visible 12-column grid, asymmetric placement, large numerals, generous but measured margins. Nothing centred.
- **Hero archetype:** `grid-asymmetric`
- **Imagery:** Flat geometric fields and cropped photography inside grid modules. No rounded corners.

## Brutalist utility (`brutalist-utility`)

- **Reference:** Hardware store shelf labels, invoices and council forms
- **Type:** Archivo Black / IBM Plex Mono; Familjen Grotesk / JetBrains Mono; Rubik Mono One / Space Mono. A heavy condensed display, a monospaced text face, uppercase labels. No decorative type.
- **Colour (raw-concrete):** Unbleached grey ground, black, and safety yellow as the only colour. Starting point: #E4E2DD, #000000, #FFD400.
- **Layout grammar:** Thick borders, visible boxes, tables and lists that look like forms. Links underlined. The price or call-out time is the hero.
- **Hero archetype:** `form-block`
- **Imagery:** None or plain product photos on white. Iconography as text labels.

## Warm craft (`warm-craft`)

- **Reference:** Workshop aprons, kraft-paper packaging and letterpress cards
- **Type:** Young Serif / Instrument Sans; Gupter / Albert Sans; Bitter / Karla. A soft, sturdy serif for headings, a humane sans for text. Generous line height.
- **Colour (kraft-earth):** Kraft and clay tones with a deep green or ink blue anchor. Warm, never beige-on-beige. Starting point: #EADBC8, #2F3B2F, #B5562C.
- **Layout grammar:** Stacked sections with hand-ruled dividers, stamped labels and process steps. One idea per band.
- **Hero archetype:** `stacked-label`
- **Imagery:** Close-ups of hands, tools and materials (the client's own only). Paper texture through CSS, never stock.

## Architectural monochrome (`architectural-monochrome`)

- **Reference:** Architecture practice monographs and gallery wall text
- **Type:** Italiana / Hanken Grotesk; Tenor Sans / Mulish; Forum / Figtree. A light, wide display at very large size, small tracked-out sans labels.
- **Colour (stone-monochrome):** Stone, graphite and white. Colour only from the client's photographs. Starting point: #F2F1EF, #2B2B2B, #8A8580.
- **Layout grammar:** Very wide margins, a strict baseline, captions and index numbers. Large single image or a single line of type.
- **Hero archetype:** `framed-specimen`
- **Imagery:** Full-bleed architectural photography or plans. Strong verticals.

## Archival heritage (`archival-heritage`)

- **Reference:** Museum catalogue cards, old company ledgers and seals
- **Type:** Libre Caslon Text / Libre Franklin; Sorts Mill Goudy / Source Sans 3; EB Garamond / Hind. A classical text serif with italic for emphasis, a sober sans for UI. Numbered sections like a catalogue.
- **Colour (oxblood-parchment):** Parchment ground, oxblood and bottle green, gold only as a thin rule. Starting point: #F1E9D8, #5A1E1B, #1F3D2B.
- **Layout grammar:** Index cards, reference numbers, dated entries and a timeline. Frames and double rules used sparingly.
- **Hero archetype:** `catalogue-card`
- **Imagery:** Archive photographs, documents and marks the client really has. Never fake old photos.

## Magazine collage (`magazine-collage`)

- **Reference:** Independent lifestyle magazines and cut-paper collage
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque / Literata; Unbounded / Newsreader; Gasoek One / Figtree. An expressive grotesque that changes weight and width, a bookish serif for text. Headlines can overlap images.
- **Colour (bright-paper):** Two bright paper colours that clash on purpose, with black. Never a gradient. Starting point: #FF8FB1, #1E6BFF, #111111.
- **Layout grammar:** Overlapping layers, rotated labels, pull quotes, cut-out shapes. Controlled chaos on a grid underneath.
- **Hero archetype:** `collage-overlap`
- **Imagery:** Cut-out photographs, torn edges and stickers built in CSS or SVG.

## Technical spec sheet (`technical-spec-sheet`)

- **Reference:** Engineering datasheets, part catalogues and wiring diagrams
- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans Condensed / IBM Plex Mono; Barlow Condensed / DM Mono; Saira Condensed / Red Hat Mono. Condensed sans for headings, monospace for specifications. Tabular figures everywhere.
- **Colour (blueprint):** Pale drafting paper, Prussian blue line work, one warning orange. Starting point: #EEF2F5, #123A6B, #F26B1D.
- **Layout grammar:** Spec tables, labelled callouts with leader lines, part numbers, revision boxes in the corner.
- **Hero archetype:** `spec-table`
- **Imagery:** Line diagrams and exploded views in inline SVG. Photos only as numbered figures.

## Hand-made signage (`hand-made-signage`)

- **Reference:** Painted shopfront signs, bakkie door lettering and market chalkboards
- **Type:** Alfa Slab One / Atkinson Hyperlegible; Rozha One / Nunito Sans; Bowlby One / Lexend. A bold slab for the sign, a very legible sans for everything else. Big, friendly, direct.
- **Colour (painted-sign):** Signwriter enamel: cream, a deep red or green panel, black outline. Starting point: #FFF6E0, #0F5C3A, #D7261E.
- **Layout grammar:** Sign panels with outlines and drop shadows, phone number as big as the headline, arrows and badges.
- **Hero archetype:** `signboard-block`
- **Imagery:** The client's van, shopfront or team at work. Painted-style borders in CSS.

## Cinematic dark (`cinematic-dark`)

- **Reference:** Film title sequences and theatre programmes
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda / Red Hat Text; Rufina / Outfit; Chonburi / Sora. High-contrast didone headlines with wide tracking, a clean sans for text. Few words, large.
- **Colour (night-ember):** Near-black ground, warm off-white type, one ember or brass accent. No neon, no purple glow. Starting point: #0D0C0B, #EDE6DA, #C8873A.
- **Layout grammar:** Letterbox bands, slow reveal of sections, credits-style lists of services.
- **Hero archetype:** `letterbox`
- **Imagery:** Moody, low-key photography of the real place or work. Grain via CSS, never stock.

## Botanical (`botanical`)

- **Reference:** Herbarium sheets and botanical illustration plates
- **Type:** Gloock / Figtree; Vollkorn / Nunito; Alike / Mukta. A characterful serif for names, a soft sans for text, Latin-style labels in italic.
- **Colour (herbarium):** Cotton-paper ground, leaf and moss greens, a pressed-flower accent. Starting point: #F6F3EA, #3E5C3A, #C2577A.
- **Layout grammar:** Specimen plates with labels, numbered figures and lots of air around a single subject.
- **Hero archetype:** `specimen-plate`
- **Imagery:** Line-drawn botanical motifs in SVG, or the client's own photography framed like a plate.

## Data-forward (`data-forward`)

- **Reference:** Annual-report infographics and transit timetables
- **Type:** Chivo / Chivo Mono; Martian Mono / Instrument Sans; Anybody / Fira Code. A strong sans with tabular numerals, mono for figures. Numbers are the headline.
- **Colour (chart-ink):** White ground, a dark slate, and a categorical set of two or three chart colours. Starting point: #FFFFFF, #1C2430, #00A38C.
- **Layout grammar:** Key figures, small charts, comparison tables and timelines up front. Only real numbers from the capture; no invented statistics.
- **Hero archetype:** `figure-strip`
- **Imagery:** Charts and diagrams in SVG built from real data, or none.

## Municipal wayfinding (`municipal-wayfinding`)

- **Reference:** Airport, hospital and transit signage systems
- **Type:** Overpass / Overpass; Red Hat Display / Red Hat Text; Encode Sans / Encode Sans. A signage sans in bold and regular, pictograms, arrows, numbered routes.
- **Colour (transit-bands):** Coloured route bands on white: one band colour per service, black text, high contrast. Starting point: #FFFFFF, #1A1A1A, #0072CE.
- **Layout grammar:** Horizontal bands, arrows to each service, step numbers. Navigation is the design.
- **Hero archetype:** `wayfinding-bands`
- **Imagery:** Pictograms in SVG. Photos inset in signage panels.

## Risograph zine (`risograph-zine`)

- **Reference:** Two-colour risograph prints and photocopied zines
- **Type:** Darker Grotesque / Courier Prime; Syne / Cousine. A tall grotesque for headlines, typewriter text. Misregistered overprint effects via CSS blend modes.
- **Colour (riso-duotone):** Exactly two ink colours on paper, overprinted for a third. Starting point: #F7F4EE, #FF48B0, #0078BF.
- **Layout grammar:** Staple-bound zine pages: page numbers, halftone panels, handwritten-style annotations (CSS, no script fonts from the web).
- **Hero archetype:** `halftone-poster`
- **Imagery:** Halftone or duotone treatment of the client's own photos via CSS filters.

## Nautical chart (`nautical-chart`)

- **Reference:** Admiralty sea charts and tide tables
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display / Source Serif 4; Oswald / Crimson Pro; League Gothic / Lora. A tall condensed display, a readable serif for text, coordinates and depth figures as accents.
- **Colour (chart-sea):** Chart paper, shallow-water blue, buoy red and lighthouse yellow in small doses. Starting point: #F4F1E8, #8EC5D6, #C3302C.
- **Layout grammar:** Contour lines and coordinates as structure, a compass-rose index, legend boxes.
- **Hero archetype:** `chart-legend`
- **Imagery:** Contour and grid patterns in SVG. Location shown honestly, never an invented service area.

## Quiet apothecary (`quiet-apothecary`)

- **Reference:** Apothecary labels and perfume-house print catalogues
- **Type:** Gilda Display / Karla; Marcellus / Jost; Cormorant / Albert Sans. A restrained display serif at a large size, set with air, and a plain text face that never competes. Very few sizes.
- **Colour (mineral-sage):** A cool mineral ground, soft black, and one oxide accent. Quiet, never beige. Starting point: #DADFD6, #1B1D1A, #7A3E1D.
- **Layout grammar:** A label-like composition with an exact margin system: one object or one sentence, and a small block of precise information set far from it.
- **Hero archetype:** `label-sheet`
- **Imagery:** One still-life or material study, lit softly, with room around it. Never a lifestyle photo.

## Kinetic poster (`kinetic-poster`)

- **Reference:** Contemporary Swiss theatre and festival posters
- **Type:** Anton / Manrope; Bebas Neue / Rubik; Big Shoulders Display / Public Sans. A condensed display at an unreasonable size, cropped by the viewport if it reads, with a calm text face.
- **Colour (single-field):** One saturated colour floods the whole hero, with black or white type. No second accent. Starting point: #E4572E, #0F0F0F, #FFFFFF.
- **Layout grammar:** Type is the image: it fills the page, moves, and locks into place. Information sits in small exact blocks at the edges.
- **Hero archetype:** `type-field`
- **Imagery:** None needed. If used, a single high-contrast cut-out overlapping the type.

## Identity manual (`identity-manual`)

- **Reference:** 1970s corporate identity manuals and signage standards
- **Type:** Archivo / Archivo; Sora / Sora; Onest / Onest. One family used with rigour: width and weight do the work, set tight and flush left.
- **Colour (black-one-hue):** Black, white and exactly one corporate hue taken from the client's brand where possible. Starting point: #101010, #F4F4F2, #C58841.
- **Layout grammar:** A strict modular system with the brand mark treated as architecture: enlarged, cropped and used as the composition's structure.
- **Hero archetype:** `mark-as-structure`
- **Imagery:** The client's mark, enlarged and cropped, without being redrawn. Architectural photography as a module, not a backdrop.

## Material study (`material-study`)

- **Reference:** Stone, metal and paper sample books from architectural suppliers
- **Type:** Syne / Newsreader; Cormorant Infant / Manrope; Tenor Sans / Newsreader. A wide, assured display against a readable text serif. Display kept short.
- **Colour (metal-and-stone):** Colours sampled from one physical material in the imagery: bronze, slate, travertine. The material is the palette. Starting point: #1F1D1B, #B8824A, #E7E2DA.
- **Layout grammar:** Large tactile surfaces in full-bleed panels, with text set directly on the material and small technical captions.
- **Hero archetype:** `material-panel`
- **Imagery:** Macro photography of a physical material, generated or real. Never people, never office interiors.
